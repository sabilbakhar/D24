The studio

Detailing is not washing.It's restoration, with patience.

A wash removes dirt. Detailing removes the evidence of time: the swirl marks from automatic brushes, the water spots baked in by the Mangaluru sun, the haze on a headlight, the salt that the coast leaves on everything. D24Studio exists for the owner who notices those things.

We work under inspection lighting, measure paint before we touch it, and quote after we've seen the car, not before. It's slower. It's also the only way to promise a result.

  • Measured

    Every panel is read with a paint depth gauge before any machine polishing. We know how much clear coat there is to work with, and we leave most of it.

  • Hand finished

    Coatings are levelled by hand, panel by panel, and cured in the bay. Nothing leaves while it's still tacky.

  • Honest

    If a scratch is through to primer, we'll say so and point you to a painter. We correct what correction can fix.

The process

Five stages.No shortcuts between them.

Detailing is a sequence, and skipping a stage shows up months later as a coating that peels or a scratch that comes back. This is the order every car goes through here.

  1. 1

    Inspection

    Paint depth read on every panel, defects mapped under LED and swirl-finder light, plan agreed with you before anything starts.

    Clear coat typical
    35 to 50 µm
  2. 2

    Decontamination

    Foam, two-bucket wash, iron remover, tar remover, clay. The surface is chemically and physically clean before a pad touches it.

    Wash chemistry
    pH neutral
  3. 3

    Correction

    Compounding and polishing in stages, checked panel by panel. We remove the least paint that gives the finish you asked for.

    Paint removed
    Under 5 µm
  4. 4

    Protection

    Panel wipe, then coating or film. Applied in a dust-controlled bay, levelled by hand, and left to cure. Nothing gets wet for a day.

    Initial cure
    24 hours
  5. 5

    Handover

    Final inspection with you, under the same lights we started with. You leave with a care sheet and a wash schedule that suits the coast.

    First maintenance
    2 weeks

Questions

Before you bookthe things owners ask us first.

Straight answers about car detailing, ceramic coating and paint protection film in Mangaluru. Anything else, send it on WhatsApp.

What is the difference between car detailing and a car wash?

A wash removes dirt. Detailing restores the surface: swirl marks, water spots and oxidation are polished out of the clear coat, then the paint is protected with a ceramic coating or film. It takes hours or days, not minutes.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

A professionally applied coating on corrected paint typically protects for two to five years, depending on the product, how the car is washed and how much sun and salt it sees. Maintenance washes every few weeks keep it performing.

How long will you keep my car?

Paint correction takes one to three days. Ceramic coating adds a 24 hour cure. Paint protection film takes two to five days depending on coverage. Interior detailing is usually a single day. We confirm timing after inspecting the car.

Can you remove deep scratches?

If the scratch is in the clear coat, yes. If your fingernail catches in it, it has usually gone through to the primer and needs paint, not polishing. We will tell you honestly at inspection.
